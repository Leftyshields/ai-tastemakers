## What this is

**Tastemakers Lab** explains how the daily digest actually works and shows the work behind every attempt to make it better.

The briefings recommend tools for search, context compaction, and agent workflows every week. The pipeline that writes those briefings should practice what it preaches: measure what readers do, test changes carefully, and publish honest results instead of shipping tweaks in the dark.

This is not a product roadmap or a marketing page. It is an open notebook for curious readers who want to know *why* a digest entry looks the way it does and *what happened* when something changed.

## What you'll find here

- **[Posts](posts/)**: short stories about each time the Lab borrowed an idea from a featured tool.
- **[Scoring guide](shadow-rubric.html)**: how "before" and "after" write-ups get scored before a change stays on.

## How to read an experiment

Each experiment states a **question** (what should improve), **what changed**, and two time periods: **before** (change off) and **after** (change on). Numbers come from site analytics pulled after each period. This page updates whenever the site rebuilds.

Experiments compare **before and after periods** rather than running two versions at once, because the site is plain web pages rebuilt daily. Feedback is slower, but every decision is easy to check later.

## Transparency boundaries

Everything in the Lab is safe to publish: no account keys, no subscriber data, no unpublished digest drafts. The pipeline's code lives on [GitHub](https://github.com/Leftyshields/ai-tastemakers), and the Lab is the readable layer on top.
