# Smarter early ranking

_**Queued, not started.** This experiment will make the Skills list less random during stretches with little star history. It is planned for Dec 20, 2026 to Jan 16, 2027._

## The idea

The Skills list ranks projects by how many GitHub stars they gained over the past seven days. That works once there are enough daily star counts to compare. During stretches with too little history, the ranking gets shaky. Giant, already famous projects and one-day spikes can crowd the top 10.

For those stretches, the Skills list already skips projects above a star limit, currently 25,000 stars. This experiment will tighten that limit, and possibly related filters, so early lists look less random without hiding projects with real momentum.

The exact new limit isn't set yet. The Lab will choose it after reviewing the lists from the first half of the test. One early idea is 20,000 stars, but that is a placeholder, not a decision.

## The change

The tighter limit will apply only on the Skills list, only during the second half of the test. The OSS list has its own limit and won't change.

| Period | Dates (Pacific time) |
|--------|----------------------|
| Before the change | Dec 20, 2026 to Jan 2, 2027 |
| With the change | Jan 3 to Jan 16, 2027 |

## How to tell if it worked

- **Steadier lists:** more of the top 10 carries over from one day to the next during low-history stretches, with fewer wild swings.
- **Still finds new things:** projects with real momentum, like new skills and timely projects, still make the list.
- **Less crowding:** fewer days where giant projects take over the top 10.

These signals come from the published daily lists. Reader clicks will be tracked but won't decide anything. Given recent Skills traffic, that's a kindness to the clicks.
